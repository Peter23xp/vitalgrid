④ Data Layer — AWS Databases
🗄️
Aurora DSQL
PostgreSQL-compatible · Serverless · ACID

Handles all transactional data — organizations, facilities, users, inventory, transfers, alerts, audit logs.

12 tables: organizations · facilities · users · resources · batches · transfers · alerts · inventory_movements · audit_log · broadcasts · alert_rules · cold_chain_events

Chosen for multi-tenant ACID transactions — when 2 facilities update the same blood stock concurrently, OCC conflicts are auto-retried. No instances to manage; scales to zero.
⚡
AWS DynamoDB
NoSQL · Key-Value · Millisecond latency

Handles IoT cold-chain events — temperature readings from sensors attached to blood transport containers.

Table: cold_chain_events
PK: transferId (String)
SK: timestamp (ISO 8601)
Attrs: celsius · deviceId · isAlert

Chosen for IoT burst writes — sensors emit readings every 5 min across 100s of active transfers. Relational overhead would be wasteful; DynamoDB absorbs the volume at sub-ms latency.
